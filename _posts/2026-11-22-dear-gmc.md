Dear GMC,

If you have done your proper due diligence, you have found this page in preparation for the POC roast video. I applaud you.

I want you to know...

I HAVE NO SHAME ABOUT ANYTHING HERE. I feel no shame about the writing on cast iron skillets, the half-filled notes where I didn't have the time to write more, and I have ZERO shame over the reviews of romance novels (which I enjoyed).

I HAVE NO SHAME ABOUT WHO I AM. I have no shame having watched several seasons of My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic and that my favorite character is Starlight Glimmer. I have no shame that I've watched The Summer I Turned Pretty and supported Conrad Fisher from the first episode... and that I finished watching it, on my own, after breaking up with my then girlfriend. I have no shame that I made a Hinge profile and deleted the app after a week and a half. I don't feel shame that I got ghosted by my matches. I won't feel shame for doing the things that make me happy or are attempts to end my loneliness.

Frankly, I feel sorry for you. That you feel so held down by society, that you cannot enjoy all that life has to offer.

**I may be weird, but you are not free.**

If you still do not understand, fucking watch the Dead Poet's Society.
