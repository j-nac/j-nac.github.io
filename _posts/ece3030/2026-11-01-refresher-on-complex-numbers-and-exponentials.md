## Complex Numbers

$$
\begin{align*}
    & j = \sqrt{-1} \\
    & j\cdot j = -1 \\
    & j = -\frac{1}{j} \\
    & e^{j\omega t} = \cos(\omega t) + j\sin(\omega t)
\end{align*}
$$

## Exponentials

$$
\begin{align*}
    & e^ae^b = e^{a+b} \\
    & e^{-a} = \frac{1}{e^a}
\end{align*}
$$
