## What is a Plasma?

A plasma is ionized gas, like a cloud of charges that are free to move around. It's like a metal conductor in that charge can easily flow through it, but it is a gas instead of a solid.

## Plasma Frequency

The key to understanding how EM waves move through plasma is the plasma frequency.

$$ \omega_p = \sqrt{\frac{Ne^2}{m\epsilon_0}} $$

At frequencies below the plasma frequency, the wave is reflected off of the plasma. At frequencies above the plasma frequency, the waves travel straight through.

The permittivity of a plasma is frequency dependent.

$$ \epsilon_r(\omega) = 1-\frac{\omega_p^2}{\omega^2} $$
